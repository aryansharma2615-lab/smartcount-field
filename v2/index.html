How it works: tap one cap of each gas colour. SmartCount finds every cap with that colour and size, and splits caps that touch. Numbered dots are the proof: tap Fix to add a missed one or remove a wrong one. Counts by colour and shape only; it does not read gas pressure. Live-camera version
