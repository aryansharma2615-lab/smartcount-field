Build order

10 steps
  1. 1Measure the three numbers that can change the designmultimeter + calipers + luggage scale · 40 minA1 pin-1 cavity · A3 magnet pull · A4 sled hole spacingA multimeter on continuity beeps when metal joins the probes. Find pin 1 BEFORE the boards are ordered.
  2. 2Order the boards — one panelised JLCPCB order, ENIGlaptop · 20 min5 designs on one 100 x 100 panel, 1.6 mm, ENIGFive panels give five complete sets: a spare receiver and a spare of every tool board.
  3. 3Print the interface parts — PETGEnder 3 S1 Pro · ~6 hno supports anywhereGroove face UP so the 90° V-grooves print as 45° walls. Dowel bosses grow up.
  4. 4Solder the receiver boardsoldering iron · 45 min2 x 824 strips — row A shoulder 0.30 mm proud of row BThe 0.30 mm is the whole safety feature. Use a shim, not an eyeball.
  5. 5Solder the four tool boardssoldering iron · 1 hID resistor per board: 10k print · 22k laser · 47k CNC · 100k bendOnly the pads that tool uses get wired. The rest are no-connect on purpose.
  6. 6Receiver: press 3 balls, glue 4 magnets, fit the boards on their dowelsvise + CA glue + 2.5 mm hex · 40 minballs flush at 3.0 mm · magnets at z ±24, polarity marked · boards on Ø2.00 bossesMagnets are SYMMETRIC now (z ±24): that is what makes push-off pure axial (D26).
  7. 7Bolt the receiver to the sled, plug the stock 24-pin cable in2.5 mm hex · 20 min4x M3x12 from the FRONT into the sled's own threadsNothing the printer came with is cut. The cable plugs into a header.
  8. 8Fit the over-centre latch2.5 mm hex · 15 minM3x16 + nylock, snug — the cam must swingFlip it and feel it go over centre. 0.385 kgf of finger for a 52.9 N clamp.
  9. 9Dock a tool and check the IDfingers + multimeter · 10 mindock-sense closed · ADC counts 2048 / 2815 / 3377 / 3723Wrong or missing ID must leave the brain in SAFE. Test that it does, by docking nothing.
  10. 10Scope the laser FET and set the jumperoscilloscope · 20 minM3 S500 — look for a 24 V square wave on pin 6 or pin 9Marlin maps laser PWM to PC0, which is the hotend-fan FET. Confirm which pin it reaches.

Measure before you commit

MEASURED = 0 of 184 params · ASSUMED 33 · ASSUMED 33 · CHOSEN 63 · DERIVED 62 · SOURCED 26
  • BRAIN_CABLE_D4 mmThe 4-wire Safety Brain cable — the ONLY thing v2 adds to the moving harness. It rides the same chain.
  • CABLE_T2 mmStock ribbon thickness.
  • CABLE_W14 mmThe stock 24-pin ribbon's width, for the strain relief and the chain.
  • CONN_FILLET0.5 mmHow far a TRIMMED XH post plus its solder fillet stands past the pad plane, into the coupling gap. Must stay under GAP = 1.74 mm with margin; the DFM gate checks it at both poses. Trim flush, then this is the fillet alone.
  • ENC_X560 mmOutside width. S1 Pro is ~487 wide incl. spool & screen.
  • ENC_Z640 mmHeight from the desk.
  • ENC_Z0-110 mmDesk height in the bed frame.
  • FFC_PITCH0.5 mmConductor pitch of the stock Sprite 24-way flat cable. 24 * 0.50 = 12.0 mm of conductors inside the SOURCED CABLE_W = 14.0 mm, which is the only standard pitch that fits — 1.00 mm would need 24 mm.
  • FFC_T0.3 mmThickness of a 0.5 mm-pitch flat flexible cable.
  • FINGER_D20 mmFinger-access envelope swept around the handle for the DFM check. A latch you cannot reach is not a latch.
  • HDR_D5 mmHeader body depth.
  • HDR_H8.5 mmHeader body height — the clearance the receiver shell must leave behind the board, plus the mated shroud.
  • HDR_W31 mm2x12 2.54 mm header body width on the BACK of the receiver board, where the printer's own unmodified cable plugs in (L17).
  • HOLE_COMP0.2 mmAdded to every printed hole; FDM holes print undersize. Replace with the tolerance-coupon result.
  • HP_D22 mmFlex-shaft handpiece nose diameter. Caliper the one bought.
  • K_CAM12000 N/mmStiffness of the cam bearing on its printed boss. The cam bears in COMPRESSION, not bending, which is why the give is 1.3 microns and not tenths.
  • K_HEAD20 N/mmToolhead stiffness. Measure: push with a luggage scale, read a dial gauge.
  • M4_INS_D5.6 mmBore for an M4 heat-set insert from the tray (caliper it).
  • M4_INS_L8 mmBore depth for M4 insert.
  • MAG_PULL_N5.975 NPull PER PAIR, magnet-to-magnet across GAP — NOT the 14.9 N contact-to-steel catalogue figure. 4 pairs = 23.9 N against 14.13 N of push-off, but the uncertainty is +/-14.3 N, which is why magnets alone were rejected. MEASURE with a luggage scale.
  • PCB_MARGIN4 mmCopper-free board edge around the pad array: room for the dowel holes, the mounting and a router tab.
  • PIN_BARREL_D1.7 mm824 strip barrel OD. Needed for the receiver through-holes. Not on the Mouser summary page — confirm on the Mill-Max drawing or with calipers.
  • PIN_FREE2.5926 mmFree (uncompressed) plunger protrusion above the 824 insulator body top. NOT on the Mouser page — but it is not free either: it must be >= PROT_A, or the row-A insulator body pokes out through the receiver face. Set equal to PROT_A, the tightest value that keeps both bodies behind the face, and > STROKE_MAX 1.40 so the plunger never bottoms. MEASURE IT: if the real strip is shorter, the receiver PCB has to move forward and the window has to open up.
  • PIN_TAIL_L3 mmThrough-hole solder tail behind the insulator body. Sets the clearance the receiver shell needs behind the PCB, next to HDR_H.
  • PIN_TIP_D0.9 mmSpring-pin plunger crown diameter. Sets how far the pad may miss before the tip walks off the copper.
  • SLED_DX32 mmS1 Pro sled M3 hole spacing across X. NOT PUBLISHED — caliper the sled before printing.
  • SLED_DZ35 mmS1 Pro sled M3 hole spacing up Z. NOT PUBLISHED — caliper first.
  • STRIP_BODY_H3 mm824 strip insulator body height. Sets how deep the strip sits and so where PROT_A/PROT_B are measured from. CONFIRM — PROUD is built on it.
  • STRIP_L30.48 mm824-22-012 strip length, 12 positions plus an end margin.
  • STRIP_W2.4 mm824 single-row insulator body width across the strip (z). Chosen just under ROW_PITCH 2.54 so the two strips sit shoulder to shoulder with 0.14 mm between them. CONFIRM — if it is wider than 2.54 the two strips interfere and ROW_PITCH has to grow.
  • TOL_REPEAT0.05 mmBall-coupling repeatability. TARGET, not yet measured — the whole stack-up is built on it. Print the coupon, 10 cycles, dial gauge.
  • VAC_D32 mmShop-vac hose OD the port takes.
  • Y_SLED30 mmFront face of the S1 Pro sled — the y datum the whole coupling hangs off. v1 hard-coded this as a bare module constant (minishop_v1.py line 104) so it never entered the ledger; v2 needs it in params.json because the PCB and pin solids are built in absolute machine y. NOT measured — v1 wrote it as a round 30.0 with the comment '(context)'. Every y pose in v2 hangs off it, so a caliper on the real sled face is the highest-leverage measurement on the list.
