The swap, electrically

10 s · by hand · no re-zeroing
  1. 1M5 · M104 S0 · M107 · M18laser off, heater off, fans off, motors released
  2. 2Latch openover-centre cam, 3.8 N finger force
  3. 3Pull offrow B (power) breaks first, row A (ground) last
  4. 4Dock next tool3 balls seat · magnets pull
  5. 5Pins compress 0.7 mmground + dock-sense touch first
  6. 6ESP32 reads tool-ID22 kΩ → LASER · LED green
